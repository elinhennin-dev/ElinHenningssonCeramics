About Elin

Rooted in place, attentive to process.

I am Elin, the maker behind Elin Henningsson Ceramics.

Working from Sweden, I create handmade pieces shaped by a fascination with form, surface, and the tactile experience of everyday objects. Each is made slowly and individually, with an appreciation for warmth, usefulness, and character.
